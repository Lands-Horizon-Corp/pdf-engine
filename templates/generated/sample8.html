Message from the CEO

A year of compounding small wins.

We set out to make document generation boring: predictable, cheap and fast. This year we moved the heavy lifting in-process, cut median render time to a few milliseconds, and passed 400 million documents. None of that matters without our customers' trust, which we treat as the real balance-sheet item.

Looking ahead, we will invest in observability, multi-region delivery and richer templates — while keeping the engine small enough for one person to understand.
